How we work together

No guessing. no wasted shoot days.

You don't have to figure out the content yourself. Tell me what you need, and I come back with a plan you can see and approve before the camera turns on.

  1. You send a brief

    Your product, your goal and any reference you like. A few lines are enough to start.

  2. I send references

    Before any shoot, I send you more than one reference so you can see the direction and approve it first.

  3. The shoot plan

    I tell you exactly what we'll film, how the shoot day will run, and why we're making each video.

  4. Shoot & edit

    I film and edit everything myself. Your final reels are ready within 3–5 days of the shoot.
